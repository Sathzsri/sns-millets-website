India's Millet Exports Reach USD 61.57 Million — APEDA 2026 Trade Insights for International Buyers

Subashri M • September 22, 2026 • 0 views

APEDA Trade Review: Record Millets Sourcing & Global Market Expansion

Fresh export figures released by the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA) reveal that India’s millet exports reached 138,690 Metric Tonnes (MT), valued at USD 61.57 Million (₹543.40 Crore). Here is what this data signals for food importers, wholesalers, and FMCG brands globally in late 2026.

India’s position as the world's leading producer and exporter of Shree Anna (Millets) is solidifying rapidly. Driven by strong consumer demand across the Middle East, North America, and Europe for gluten-free, low-GI ancient grains, Indian exporters are delivering unprecedented volume across both raw Sortex grains and processed functional foods.

1. Top Export Destinations (APEDA 2026 Data)

According to official trade metrics, Indian millet shipments are concentrated in high-demand international commercial markets:

2. Product Demand Breakdown by Millet Variety

The APEDA trade analysis highlights how distinct millet varieties fulfill specific global market segments:

  • Pearl Millet (Bajra) — 40% Global Share: Remains the highest-volume export variety due to its commercial scale, competitive pricing, and widespread use in both food processing and animal feed.
  • Finger Millet (Ragi): Driving strong growth in North America and Europe for low-glycemic, calcium-rich infant foods and gluten-free bakery mixes.
  • Small Millets (Foxtail, Kodo, Barnyard, Browntop): Experiencing the fastest percentage growth in premium functional food product lines and diabetic-friendly meals.

3. The 2026 Global Market Forecast

Industry analysts project the global millet market to expand from USD 13.22 Billion in 2026 to USD 16.78 Billion by 2031. Overseas buyers who establish reliable long-term supply agreements with certified Indian exporters right now are locking in consistent pricing and guaranteed grain purity before upcoming seasonal spikes.
