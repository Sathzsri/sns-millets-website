India Ships Millet Foods to New Zealand by Sea – First Export Shipment Highlights Growing Market

By Subashri M, Proprietor, SNS Global Traders | November 3, 2026

India’s first sea shipment of millet functional foods to New Zealand in 2026 opens a new export corridor, showcasing the reliability of Indian millets for Pacific markets. The 1‑metric‑ton consignment, handled by APEDA‑certified exporters, demonstrates our capacity to meet growing demand for sustainable, gluten‑free grain alternatives.

Key highlights:

  • First‑ever sea freight of ready‑to‑cook millet products to New Zealand.
  • Supported by APEDA certification and strict quality controls.
  • Strengthens bilateral trade ties and paves the way for larger future shipments.

Buyers looking to diversify their product lines with nutritious, low‑glycemic millets can now source directly from India, benefitting from transparent documentation and consistent supply.
