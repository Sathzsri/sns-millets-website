The World's #1 Most Traded Millet Variety

Accounting for over 40% of the total global millet market, Pearl Millet (known as Bajra in Hindi and Kambu in Tamil) is the cornerstone of commercial grain procurement. Here is a breakdown of export specifications, Sortex cleaning, and FCL container logistics.

Pearl Millet (Pennisetum glaucum) is celebrated worldwide for its climate resilience, high iron and magnesium content, and versatility in both human food production (flours, extrudates, bakery) and specialized animal feed formulations.
